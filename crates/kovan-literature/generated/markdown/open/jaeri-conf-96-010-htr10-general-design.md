### 3.4 General Design of the 10MW HTR

Zhenya Qin

Institute of Nuclear Energy Technology Tsinghua University, Beijing, China

## Abstract:

According to the development programme of advanced technologies in

China, a 10MW side-by-side high temperature gas-cooled test module reactor

with spherical fuel elements will be erected before the year 2000 by the Institute

of Nuclear Energy Technology of Tsinghua University. The aims of this project

are to demonstrate the inherent safety features of the HTGR modular design and

to test the technologies of electricity generation, district heating as well as

process heat application with modular HTGR.

At present, the 10MW HTR main building is being constructed and the

principle large components have been ordered and are being fabricated.

The design philosophy and safety features, the reactor structure, the

principle components, the design parameters, the general layout of principle

systems in the nuclear island of the 10MW high temperature gas-cooled test

module will be presented in this paper.

## Abstract

According to the plan of advanced technology development of our county, a 10MW High

Temperature Gas cooled Test reactor (HTR-10) was designed by INET of Tsinghua University. At

present, the reactor building is being constructed and the principal components are being fabricated

or under goods. This paper describes the technical design of the HTR-10 test reactor.

## 1. General description

The Fig. 1 shows the HTR-10 primary system. The primary pressure boundary consists of

reactor pressure vessel, steam generator pressure vessel and hot gas duct pressure vessel which

connects the above two vessels. This arrangement can make the maintenance and inspection of the

components easier and mitigate the accident result of water ingress into reactor core if the steam

generator tubes were broken. We hope that the inherent safety features of side-by-side style HTR

module can be demonstrated.

## 2. Reactor

The reactor core volume is 5m 3, 1.8m in diameter and the mean height is 1.97m. About

27,000 spherical fuel elements with 60mm in diameter will be filled up in the reactor core, the

enrichment of fuel is 17% and the mean burn up is 80,000MWd/T. At the funnel bottom of the

reactor core, there is a fuel elements discharging tube, about 3.3m long and 500mm in diameter, at

its end part through a special discharge facility the tube becomes a smaller one with 65mm in

diameter only and penetrates the reactor pressure vessel. The fuel elements would be discharge one

by one into the discharge system during the normal operation condition, therefor the shielding and

cooling of the discharge tube will be easy. At the top of reactor core there is one loading tube, the

internal diameter is 62mm. -150 -

Around the reactor core there are graphite reflectors, they can be divided into three parts:

top reflector, side reflector and bottom reflector. In the outside of graphite reflector, there are

carbon bricks containing thermal neutron absorber materials (B4C), therefor they have thermal

insulation and absorbing functions.

The whole graphite reflector consists of 20 layers of graphite bricks and carbon bricks. Each

layer is divided into 20 segments, the graphite key and reactor core sleeve put them all together.

In the side reflector near the core there are 10 bore holes for control rods, the 10 control

rods serve as the first shutdown system of HTR-10. According to our safety authority requirement

there are 7 bore holes for small boron absorber balls as independent second shutdown system.

The reactor pressure vessel is more than ll m height and more than 4m in diameter, the

reactor total weight is more than 300t.

## 3. Steam generator

The steam generator is once through modular helical tubes style. There are 30 small helical

tubes modules which are installed in the annular space, each tube is 34m long, the total heat transfer

area is 56m 2 (See Fig. 2). The feed water temperature is 104<c, the temperature of outlet steam is

4401 , the steam pressure is 4MPa. At the center, a N2-He intermediate heat exchanger (MX) will

be installed in the second experimental stage for gas-turbine and process heat application experiment

and in this case the helium inlet and outlet temperature would be from first stage 250<C and 700^

increased to 300 t and 900T: respectively, but at the first stage the place for the IHX is empty. The

pressure vessel is more than 1 lm height and 2.6m in diameter.

the center tube with 300mm in diameter, the hot helium will flow from reactor core to steam

generator through this tube, so between the inside tube and outside tube a thermal insulation will

## 5. The flow of coolant

The flow of the primary helium coolant is as following: After through the helium circulator,

the pressure of the cold helium would be increased about 0.6 bar and flow through the annular space

between the steam generator pressure vessel and the sleeve, that location in the outside of outside

thermal insulation, and then come into the annular tube of the hot gas duct and then through the

space between the reactor pressure vessel and the reactor core sleeve into the bottom part of the

reactor support structure, the reactor support structure will be cooled at first, about 2% of cold

coolant will be flowed into the control rods holes and 1% into the outside of fuel elements discharge

tube for cooling, most of them will be risen through the 20 cold Helium bore holes enters the top

cold helium plenum, after through the pebble bed, the cold helium was heated and from the hot

Helium bore holes flow down to the hot Helium plenum, after mixture the average temperature will

be 700^ , then through the central tube of the hot gas duct into the steam generator. After heat

exchange the helium is cooled down from 7001: to 250cc, then through 6 tubes enters the inlet of

the helium circulator to form a closed circulation (See Fig. 3).

## 6. HTR-10 Confinement

Because the ceramic coated particles of fuel are selected in HTR-10, in our case under the

normal operation condition and all accident conditions, the maximum temperature of the fuel

elements are much more lower than the 1600ic maximum temperature limit, so the coated particles

are a strong barrier against release of radioactivity, and the HTR-10 has a large negative reactivity

temperature coefficient of both fuel and moderator, its value is about -8.5x10" 5/K. So under the

normal operation condition, the maximum fuel elements temperature has a 700 <c margin to the

limits of 1600^. China nuclear safety authority agrees that for HTR-10 the containment is

unnecessary, but we have a confinement design. The primary cavity is a principal part of confinement

and the helium purification cavity is another part of confinement, therefor the primary system and all

helium systems which are connected with primary system are covered by the confinement.

The primary cavity includes reactor cavity, steam generator cavity and fuel elements

discharge system cavity. The wall thickness of reactor cavity is 2.3m mainly for shielding purpose.

The design pressure of confinement is 1.3bar, during normal operation condition, the primary cavity

is kept -150Pa lower other parts of reactor building through negative pressure system of the

ventilation system, therefore the outside atmosphere can be released into the cavity, but the cavity

inside gas couldn't release to the outside and only can be exhausted over 40m height chimney of

ventilation center after filter.

If the primary lost pressure accident happened, for example, the helium purification system

inlet and outlet tube or fuel elements discharge tube with 65mm in diameter one was damaged, the

primary coolant will release very quickly and the pressure of primary cavity will be increased, when

the pressure increased to 1.1 bar the flap or blast membrane will be opened, the gas will be

exhausted form special chimney directly into atmosphere very quickly without filter (See Fig. 4).

In the reactor cavity a passive residual heat removal system is designed. It consists of

reactor cavity coolers, air coolers and natural circulation loop. At normal shutdown condition the

helium circulator and the steam generator can be operated, therefor the residual heat can be

removed through the steam generator to the starting and shutdown loop, then to atmosphere. If

above system was failed and at the starting and shutdown loop, then to atmosphere. If above system

was failed and at the same time de pressurization accident was happened, the residual heat can be

removed passively by means of conductivity of graphite reflector, reactor pressure vessel and then

by means of radiation to the reactor cavity cooler, the residual heat will be removed passively over

natural circulation, circulation loop, air cooler to the atmosphere.
