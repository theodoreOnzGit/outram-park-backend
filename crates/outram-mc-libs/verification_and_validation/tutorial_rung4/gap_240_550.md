# Chasing the 2.40 cm gap on the default route (GitHub #550, 2026-11-19)

Research, education and V&V only. Predictions were committed before any swap:
`gap_240_550_predictions.md` (commit `a69f7997f`). Raw per-seed CSVs, the
analysis script and the cross-section dump are in `gap_240_550/`
(`analyse.py` run with `python -I`).

**Result in one paragraph.** The 2.40 cm miss of the default route against
OpenMC (-85 +- 38 pcm, z -2.26, 8 seeds) **was a statistical fluctuation of
those 8 seeds**. With 24 seeds the default route reads 0.70642 +- 0.00020,
**-10 +- 28 pcm (z -0.37)** against OpenMC (32 seeds), inside the |z| <= 2 gate.
Independently of the transport runs, the premise of the hunt (that the port's
RECONR/BROADR neutron data differ from OpenMC's `.h5`) is **false for this
tier**: every cross section, the URR probability tables, the 0 K elastic table
for resonance scattering, nu-bar, the fission spectrum, the elastic angular
law and the free-gas kT of all 11 nuclides agree to 1e-6 or better. There is no
neutron-data route difference to find, no port defect, and no option
difference of consequence. Nothing in the library was changed.

## Step 1: the premise check

Protocol of the earlier arms: case-1 borated water, 11 nuclides, 5000 x
[50 + 200], 2.40 cm, 293.6 K. New runs: seeds 9-24 of the default route and of
arm E (all eleven nuclides from OpenMC's `.h5`; both with the node-grid
S(a,b)), the same example, flags and settings as seeds 1-8
(`gap_240_550/def_s9.csv`, `E_s9.csv`; seeds 1-8 are the existing
`flip_550/pitch_flip.csv` and `sab_reference_550/arms/E_h5_nodegrid.csv`).
OpenMC reference: 32 seeds, 0.70652 +- 0.00020 (`sab_reference_550.md`).
Hardware: Intel Xeon @ 2.10 GHz, 4 logical cores, 15 GB RAM, Linux 6.18.44,
CPU only, a VM shared with other agents (load average 7-11), 2 threads per
process, two processes at a time; per-seed time 3-6 min, loading 7-8 min for
the tape route (U-235 and U-238 reconstruction, 190-220 s each) and 35 s for
the `.h5` route. Timings indicative only.

| default (tape data, node-grid S(a,b)) | 1-8 | 0.70568 +- 0.00024 | 68 | -85 +- 38 | -2.26 |
| default | 9-24 | 0.70679 | | | |
| **default** | **1-24** | **0.70642 +- 0.00020** | 96 | **-10 +- 28** | **-0.37** |

- E minus default over 24 seeds: **-15 +- 29 pcm (z -0.53)** unpaired,
  -15 +- 24 pcm (t -0.62) paired by seed. The 8-seed figure the hunt started
  from, +83 +- 46 (z +1.8), splits as seeds 1-8 +83 +- 46 and seeds 9-24 -64 +-
  33 (z -1.9): the sign flips, which is what a fluctuation does.
- The first 8 default seeds are the low block: default seeds 1-8 mean 0.70568
  against 0.70679 for seeds 9-24 (-111 pcm, about 2.6 sigma of a difference of
  block means at the 96 pcm seed sd). Arms with bitwise-equivalent data run on
  the same seeds 1-8 read 0.70651 (E), 0.70655 (H-1, O-16 swapped, below) and
  0.70641 (uranium and Mn-55 swapped, below). I looked at this block because it
  was low, so "one block of 8 out of three in 24 is 2.6 sigma low" is a
  post-hoc look and I treat it as a fluctuation, not a finding. Whether those 8
  recorded runs are reproducible is checked in step 5.
- **Disclosure:** I added seeds 9-24 because the 8-seed result was in tension
  with the 8-seed E arm. A longer ensemble is the pre-planned premise check
  (`gap_240_550_predictions.md`, Plan 1), and its outcome (E minus default
  -15 +- 29, prediction was +35 +- 25) missed my regression-to-the-mean
  prediction on the high side: it regressed all the way, and past zero.
- Pooled estimate for the default-equivalent data (default 24 + E 24 seeds,
  bitwise-equivalent inputs, see step 3): 0.70634 +- 0.00014, **-18 +- 25 pcm
  (z -0.73)**. That pooling is justified only by step 3.

**Which number to quote** for the default route at 2.40 cm: the 24-seed
**0.70642 +- 0.00020 (-10 +- 28 pcm vs OpenMC, z -0.37)**. The 8-seed -85 +- 38
is superseded (it is a subset of these 24).

## Step 2: the mixing mechanism

Searched first: `Nuclide::from_endf_file_with_speed` (tape) and
`Nuclide::from_hdf5` (`.h5`) are the only two loaders, and
`lct008_pitch_sweep::load_nuclides` chose between them for the whole tier;
no per-nuclide mixing existed in `examples/` or `src/`. Added to the example
(no library code): `--hdf5-dir DIR --hdf5-nuclides A,B` loads only the listed
nuclides from the `.h5` and every other nuclide from its ENDF tape
(`nuclide_from_hdf5`, `parse_nuclide_list`, test
`route_tests::hdf5_nuclide_list_selects_exactly_the_listed`, run with
`cargo test --release -p outram-mc-libs --features endf-pebble-cases --example
lct008_pitch_sweep`). The S(a,b) is the same node-grid ENDF-built table in every
arm. The OpenMC S(a,b) is not involved.

## Step 3: the neutron data, compared before they were swapped

Instrument: `examples/xs_port_vs_h5_550.rs` (committed; methodology in its doc
comment). For each of the 11 nuclides it builds the tape route (RECONR + BROADR
at 293.6 K, URR + DBRC defaults) and the `.h5` route (`294K` cross sections,
`0K` elastic) and compares, on a grid of 2000 points per decade over 1e-5 eV to
20 MeV in 8 bands, the lethargy-weighted band mean and the largest pointwise
relative difference of total, elastic, capture and fission. Raw output:
`gap_240_550/xs_port_vs_h5_full.txt`.

## Step 4: the per-nuclide swaps (predictions P1-P6 against results)

Given step 3, a swap moves the same numbers by at most 1e-6 and k can differ
only through divergence of the random walk. The swaps were still run, as the
test of that: groups, because 12 seeds cannot resolve ~25 pcm for a single
nuclide (sigma of a difference to the 24-seed default arm is about 30 pcm
unpaired). Both groups use seeds 1-12, node-grid S(a,b), the rest of the tier
from the tape.

| arm | seeds | k | minus default (24 seeds), unpaired | minus E (24), unpaired | minus OpenMC | predicted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-1 and O-16 from `.h5` (P1 + P2) | 12 | 0.70654 +- 0.00024 | **+12 +- 31 pcm (z +0.38)** | +27 +- 32 | +2 +- 32 (z +0.05) | -5 (-40 .. +30) plus 0 (-15 .. +15): **held** |
| U-234, U-235, U-238, Mn-55 from `.h5` (P3 + P4 + part of P6) | 12 | 0.70681 +- 0.00033 | **+39 +- 39 pcm (z +1.01)** | +54 +- 39 | +29 +- 39 (z +0.74) | 0 (-25 .. +30) plus 0 (-20 .. +20) plus 0: **held**, central value higher than predicted, not resolved |

Paired-by-seed differences of these arms against the default on seeds 1-12
read +63 +- 45 (t +1.4) and +90 +- 40 (t +2.3) pcm. They are **not quoted**:
the default's seeds 1-8 are the low block described above, so a pairing that
over-weights those seeds measures the block, not the swap. Unpaired against the
whole 24-seed default is the figure that uses all the data.
**Not run:** B-10 alone (P5), and Al, Si-28/29/30 (P6 remainder), H-1 and O-16
separately, U-235 or U-238 separately. Not run because step 3 shows they are
not different data, and a 12-seed arm cannot resolve a 25 pcm effect on a
single nuclide anyway.

Joint prediction (E minus default = +35 +- 25): measured **-15 +- 29 pcm** at 24
seeds. **Missed**, by 1.3 sigma, in the direction of zero and beyond. The
refutation conditions I wrote down ((a) a single swap over 50 pcm with a joint
under 30; (b) E minus default under 20 at 24 seeds) are: (a) not triggered, (b)
**triggered: the premise dissolved and the 8-seed miss was a fluctuation of
the default arm.**

Prior stated before the swaps: fluctuation of the default arm's 8 seeds 35 %;
real route difference in B-10/H-1 xs 25 %; 294 K versus 293.6 K 15 %; U
resonance treatment 10 %; other 15 %. The measured result is the first, with
the second to fourth excluded by step 3 independently of k.

## Step 5: reproducibility of the recorded 8-seed default block

The recorded runs were made with the example's default thread count, in a
process that looped three pitches; this record's runs use `--threads 2` and one
pitch. Re-running default seeds 1 and 2 at 2.40 cm alone reproduced the
recorded `k_inf` **to every printed digit** (0.705216 and 0.704767, 6 digits;
`gap_240_550/def_repro.csv`). So the recorded block of seeds 1-8 is exactly what
the code gives for those seeds, independent of thread count and of what ran
before in the process; it is a low draw of the random-number streams for these
seeds, not an artefact of how those runs were launched. Seeds 3-8 were not
re-run.

## What this does not show

- It does not show the port and OpenMC agree to better than the statistics: the
  24-seed default is -10 +- 28 pcm, so differences of order +-50 pcm at 2.40 cm
  are not excluded. The earlier -85 +- 38 is not excluded at 1.5 sigma of the
  new figure either; it is simply not supported by 16 further seeds.
- It does not re-measure 1.63576 and 1.75 cm (they passed with 8-16 seeds and
  were not part of this brief), the 36-nuclide tier, or any other pitch.
- It does not touch OpenMC's own variance: the OpenMC side is the 32-seed
  mean; E is not paired with it.
- It does not explain why the first 8 default seeds are low (2.6 sigma block
  of means); a fluctuation of this size in one of the three blocks I looked at
  is not unexpected, but it is not proven to be a fluctuation either, only that
  the recorded runs do not reproduce at 16 further seeds.
