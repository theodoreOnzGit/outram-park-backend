# Routes 4 and 5 with H in H2O from LEAPR (2026-10-14)

> Research, education and V&V only.

**Data:** `per_seed_keff_leapr_routes45_2026_10_14.csv`, 128 rows. Not merged
into `per_seed_keff.csv` or `summary_keff.md`.

## Methodology

- **What changed:** at `6e35113e2`, H in H2O on route 4 is regenerated by
  LEAPR from the ENDF/B-VIII.0 deck. On route 5 it comes from
  `write_ace_library --thermal-leapr` instead of `tsl-HinH2O.endf`. Only the
  cases with water can move: HST-009 and LCT-008.
- **Run:** `run_all.sh` at `6e35113e2`, `ROUTES="4 5"`, 32 seeds, 36 threads,
  on an Intel Xeon W-2195 (Linux Mint 21.3).
  - HST-009 is 5,000 × [40 + 120].
  - LCT-008 (the case-1 lattice, 11 nuclides) is 10,000 × [250 + 400].
  - This machine has no NJOY2016, so the copy of the launcher used here
    skipped the NJOY2016 library step. Routes 1 and 3 were not run.
- **Route 2 not run:** the system OpenMC 0.14.1-dev has no `constraints=`
  argument on `IndependentSource`, so `icsbep_openmc.py` failed on every seed.
- **Reference:** the recorded route 1 and the benchmark k in
  `summary_keff.md`.
- **Pass criterion:** within 2σ of route 1.
- **Physics:** every row has URR on 4 nuclides, DBRC on 11, and S(α,β)
  attached.

**Interpretation:**
- Every case and route is within 2σ of route 1. None moves from its earlier
  record by more than about 1σ.
- That fits the LEAPR table storing the same S(α,β) as the published tape
  (`tests/thermal_h2o_leapr_vs_tsl.rs`).
- LCT-008 route 4, at −1.8σ, is the widest difference. Its 32 seeds do not
  resolve a miss; more seeds would.
