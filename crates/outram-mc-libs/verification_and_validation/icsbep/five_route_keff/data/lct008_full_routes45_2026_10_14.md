# LCT-008 on its full nuclide list, routes 4 and 5 (2026-10-14)

> Research, education and V&V only.

**Data:** `per_seed_keff_lct008_full_routes45_2026_10_14.csv`, 16 rows (case
`lct008-full`). Not merged into `per_seed_keff.csv` or `summary_keff.md`.

## Methodology

- **What is run:** LEU-COMP-THERM-008 case 1, the lattice, on its **full
  nuclide list** (35 nuclides of the case-1 cards; the model since
  2026-10-14, maintainer direction). H in H₂O comes from **LEAPR** on both
  routes:
  - route 4 (outram-mc, Rust NJOY, ENDF read directly):
    `ThermalScattering::from_embedded_leapr_deck`;
  - route 5 (outram-mc, Rust-NJOY ACE): `write_ace_library --thermal-leapr`,
    with all 36 tapes of `nuclides.tsv` in the library.
- **Run:** `icsbep_five_route_keff` at `da354c70ba`, 10 000 × [250 + 400], seeds
  1–8 on each route, 4 threads. Intel Xeon @ 2.80 GHz, 4 logical cores,
  16 GB, Linux, CPU only. The machine was shared with a headless-browser
  check during route 4's first seed. About 400 s per seed.
- **Not run:** routes 1–3. Routes 1 and 2 need OpenMC; route 3 needs the
  NJOY2016 library. This container has neither (#874).
- **Predictions, written before the run** (from the 11-nuclide LEAPR record,
  `leapr_routes45_2026_10_14.md`, minus the 24 extra nuclides' −75 ± 14 pcm
  from #533):
  - route 4 ≈ 1.00146 ± 0.00018;
  - route 5 ≈ 1.00159 ± 0.00017;
  - route 5 − route 4 within 2σ of the 11-nuclide +13 ± 16 pcm.
- **Physics in every row:** URR on 10 of 35 nuclides, DBRC on 35, S(α,β)
  attached. The only per-nuclide difference between the routes' printed
  physics is Fe-58's URR upper bound: 3.0 MeV from ENDF, 1.0 MeV from its
  ACE table.

## Results

| route | n | k ± sem | seed sd [pcm] | vs prediction [pcm] | vs benchmark 1.0007 [pcm] |

| 4 | 8 | 1.00118 ± 0.00019 | 54 | −28 ± 26 (−1.1σ) | +48 ± 19 |
| 5 | 8 | 1.00175 ± 0.00031 | 88 | +16 ± 36 (+0.4σ) | +105 ± 31 |

**Route 5 − route 4:**
- unpaired: **+58 ± 36 pcm (1.6σ)**;
- paired by seed: +58 ± 22 pcm. Pairing is only valid if the same seed
  correlates across the two data routes; that is not established, so quote
  the unpaired value.
- The 11-nuclide campaign gave +13 ± 16 pcm. The new difference is 45 ± 39
  pcm from that, inside the 2σ predicted.

**Interpretation:**
- Both routes meet their predictions. The full model sits about 100 pcm
  below the 11-nuclide one on route 4 (1.00118 against 1.00221 ± 0.00012
  with LEAPR). That is −103 ± 22 pcm, against #533's −75 ± 14 (1.1σ apart).
- Eight seeds per route do not resolve a route-4/route-5 difference of
  this size. Route 5's spread (88 pcm) is also wider than route 4's
  (54 pcm), and its first two seeds were high.
- The next step is more seeds, or localising the difference nuclide by
  nuclide with `--endf-nuclides` on route 5, if it persists. Routes 1–3 and
  the full campaign are #874.
