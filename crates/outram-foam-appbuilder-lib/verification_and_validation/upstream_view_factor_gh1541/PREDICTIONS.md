# Predictions: viewFactor radiation coupled into the CHT interface (gh:#1541), written BEFORE any run

Date 2026-11-14. Research, education and V&V only. Committed before the ESI reference was generated and before
the port-side coupling was run.

Case. The gh:#1363 two-region case (fluid 20x5x1 water 0.02 x 0.01 x 0.001 m, solid 10x5x1, dt 0.1 s, 50 steps,
left 300 K, solid wall 360 K) with ONE change in the fluid: `frontAndBack` is a `wall` patch instead of `empty`,
so the fluid boundary is a closed 3-D box (left 5 + interface 5 + walls 40 + frontAndBack 200 = 250 faces) and
ESI's `viewFactorsGen` takes its 3-D branch (the 2-D branch is not ported). The fluid carries
`radiationModel viewFactor` (direct LU, no smoothing, variable emissivity, no agglomeration), emissivities
left 0.9, interface 0.8, walls 0.5, frontAndBack 0.6, qro 0. The interface conditions carry `qr` (fluid
`qr qr; qrNbr none`, solid `qr none; qrNbr qr`). Fluid T at frontAndBack is zeroGradient. The port: F and
globalFaceFaces generated by `generate_view_factors` from the fluid polyMesh, `ViewFactorCoupling` attached to
the fluid `EnergyEquation`, `qr` into `CoupledTemperature` as for P1.

1. F, globalFaceFaces regenerated by Rust from the fluid polyMesh are byte-identical to what ESI wrote: 65 %.
   (Convex box, thin cells; the 8x8x1 plates passed, but aspect ratios here reach ~20:1 within a face
   and 2AI/2LI order selection could differ.) If not, the lists differ only in same-plane pairs (cf. gh:#1564).
2. qr on the patches at step 1 (the first `correct()`, T uniform apart from the interface and solid) is
   bit-identical to ESI when F is identical: 60 %. Because the port's T solve differs from ESI's at ~1e-11 K
   (gh:#1363's T-form vs h-form floor), qr after step 1 will agree to ~4 dT/T ~ 1e-13 relative rather than
   to the bit: so I predict NOT bit-identical from step 1 on, worst relative qr difference over 50 steps
   (relative to max |qr| of the step) <= 1e-9, fluid cell T <= 5e-11 K, solid <= 2e-11 K.
3. The radiation matters: the port without the radiative flux (ablation) differs from ESI-with-viewFactor by
   more than 1e-5 K in the fluid at step 50 and by less than 1 K. (Order-of-magnitude guess: qr is up to a few
   tens of W/m2 against a conduction flux of order 100-400 W/m2 through a 1 cm slab.)
4. Closed-enclosure energy balance of the ESI qr dump: sum(A q)/sum(A |q|) within 3 % (F row sums are ~1.01,
   F not exactly reciprocal, cf. gh:#1529 prediction 5).
5. Off by default: an `EnergyEquation` without `with_view_factor_radiation` is bit for bit what it was; the
   existing gh:#1531 P1 differential and gh:#1363 energy tests still pass unchanged.
6. P1 and viewFactor are mutually exclusive on one equation (attaching the second is refused), as in ESI
   (one `radiationModel` per region).
