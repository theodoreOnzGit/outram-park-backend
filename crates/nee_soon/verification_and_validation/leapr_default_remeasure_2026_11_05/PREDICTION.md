Written 2026-11-05, committed before the first run.

## Why

Since 2026-11-03 (gh:#1137, maintainer: "just use LEAPR by default") the
graphite (30P), C-in-SiC and Si-in-SiC thermal scattering laws are generated by
LEAPR at the run's temperature, where every record before then read the
published ENDF/B-VIII.0 tapes at their nearest temperature. Those records now
describe the ablation `OUTRAM_HTR10_TAPE_LAWS=1`, not the default model.

## Design: paired arms

Each case runs twice on the same commit, the same seed and the same
statistics: **LEAPR** (the default) and **TAPE** (`OUTRAM_HTR10_TAPE_LAWS=1`,
which the HTTR path also honours). The difference `LEAPR - TAPE` is the law
change alone (gh:#1143 item 1); correlated sampling makes it far tighter than
either `k`. The uncorrelated sigma of the difference is quoted (an upper
bound). The TAPE arm against the old record measures everything else that has
changed in the code since that record (PETIR routing, gh:#985 and later; the
2026-11 transport changes), and is reported but not attributed.

Statistics are each record's own, so the new LEAPR `k` replaces the old one
directly: HTR-10 10 000 x [5 + 135], seed 20260917 (the 2026-10-07 10k record);
HTTR 20 000 x [100 + 300], seed 20261016 (the start-up core record).

## What the change consists of (gh:#1140)

LEAPR reproduces the tapes' MF7/MT4 exactly at every tabulated temperature
(36 temperatures, 60 000 values each). What remains:

1. **Graphite's law at 300.15 K instead of the tape's 296 K** (+4.15 K). A
   warmer law gives a slightly warmer thermal spectrum.
2. **SiC's elastic channel**: coherent, from the 3C-SiC crystal data, -3.03 %
   from the tape's elastic cross section at the thermal point
   (`crates/njoy-outram-park-fork/docs/leapr-sic-coherent-elastic-vv.md`).
   SiC is one TRISO coating, a small fraction of the core's carbon.

## Predictions

| Quantity | Predicted | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| HTR-10 N = 12, `LEAPR - TAPE` | **-40 pcm, range -100 to +20** | Graphite's law 4.15 K warmer. A cold-core moderator coefficient of order -10 pcm/K, most of it carried by the scattering law, gives -40; the SiC term below 10 pcm either way |
| HTTR critical, `LEAPR - TAPE` | **-40 pcm, range -100 to +20** | Same mechanism; also a cold (~300 K) graphite core |
| HTTR subcritical, `LEAPR - TAPE` | same range as critical | the law change does not depend on the rods |
| TAPE arm against its old record | within 2 sigma of the record | unless code changes since then moved it; if it does not, that is reported, not explained away |
| DEM-bed point and rod curves | shift by the N = 12 `LEAPR - TAPE` within their sigma | same core, same law change; the rod worth (a difference of two `k`) should not move beyond its sigma |

**What would surprise me:** a `LEAPR - TAPE` outside -150 to +50 pcm on any
case, or of opposite sign between HTR-10 and HTTR. Either is a finding to
chase (the laws should be close to identical), not one to explain away.
