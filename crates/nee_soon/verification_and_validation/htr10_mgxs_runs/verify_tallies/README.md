# HTR-10 MGXS case `verify_tallies`

> **Research, education and V&V only.** Not for reactor operation, licensing or safety decisions (`RESPONSIBLE_USE.md`).

Generated by `cargo run --release -p nee_soon --example htr10_mgxs_case -- --case verify_tallies` (`nee_soon::htr10_mgxs`). Do not edit by hand; rerun instead.

**Purpose:** check the tallies on the full core at a budget a 4-core machine finishes in about an hour: tallied production/absorption against k_eff, Sigma_f <= Sigma_a <= Sigma_t, nu-bar, balance Feeds #845.

## Methodology

- **Model:** full HTR-10 core, Şeker 13-ball bed N = 12 (123.576 cm), explicit TRISO, explicit reflector, rods withdrawn.
- **Temperature:** every material at 300.15 K (isothermal state point).
- **Data:** EndfB8, graphite S(a,b) Reactor30P, thermal Bound, carbon Natural, coolant Helium, rod metal FULL: real Ni-58/60/61/62/64 and Fe-54/56/57/58
- **Data:** TECDOC-1382 reflector zone 22, natural boron (benchmark default materials)
- **Data:** carbon: natural, C-12 / C-13 at 98.93 / 1.07 at.% (IUPAC)
- **Data:** coolant: natural helium (He-3 1.343e-6 at.), ideal gas at 300.15 K and 101.33 kPa (pressure ASSUMED atmospheric, after Seker & Colak 2003 p.267)
- **Data:** rod metal: FULL: real Ni-58/60/61/62/64 and Fe-54/56/57/58
- **Data:** delta-tracked bed tallied with the TentativeCollision estimator (gh:#598)
- **Transport:** `outram_mc_libs` hybrid tracker (delta tracking in the bed, surface tracking elsewhere), 4000 particles × \[10 inactive + 40 active\] generations, seed 20261012, 4 threads.
- **Tallies:** two passes at the same seed (byte-identical histories): `[Material, Energy]` with `Flux, Total, Absorption, NuFission, KappaFission, Fission`, and `[Material, Energy, EnergyOut]` with the analog scatter matrix and the fission births. In the delta-tracked bed the flux and reaction rates come from the tentative-collision estimator (gh:#598).
- **Condensation:** one zone per material, flux-weighted, on the 70-group tally structure (NJOY WIMS-69 plus 10 to 20 MeV), then collapsed exactly onto each structure below. Nothing is rebalanced or adjusted; the library in `record.json` is the raw condensation.
- **Not done here:** no multigroup `k_eff` (needs #848 and #682), no σ on collapsed constants (the replica case measures spread), P0 scattering only, no delayed-neutron data.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 8g-wims | 8 | 0.991568 | 6.43e-4 | 5.01e0 |
| 70g | 70 | 0.991568 | 1.65e-3 | 6.43e1 |

`k_inf` is production over absorption summed over every zone, with no leakage, so it sits above `k_eff` by the core's leakage. It must be identical on every structure (collapse is exact). The balance residual is `|Sigma_a + Sigma_s,row - Sigma_t| / Sigma_t`; the collision-weighted one is the number to quote, the worst bin is whichever (zone, group) saw fewest collisions. A weighted residual well above statistics is the ν-scatter excess of #682 or a condensation defect.
