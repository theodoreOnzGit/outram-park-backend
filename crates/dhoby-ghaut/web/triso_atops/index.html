The TRISO fuel particle as OUTRAM PARK's boon-lay crate models it: its layers, decay, the Walk-on-Spheres walk, fuel failure, chemistry and release, computed in a background worker in this tab, one rung of the TRISO-ATOPS lessons at a time.

Research, education and V&V only. Not for reactor operation, licensing, safety decisions or emergency response.

Methods and data: fission-product transport in TRISO particles follows Jiang et al. (Jiang et al., 2023), the nuclear data are ENDF/B-VIII.0 (Brown & others, 2018), and the HTR design data are from IAEA-TECDOC-1382 (International Atomic Energy Agency, 2003).
