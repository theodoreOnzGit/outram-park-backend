Then real ENDF/B-VIII.0 nuclear data are downloaded and processed in this tab by OUTRAM PARK's own NJOY port, in a background worker so the page stays responsive. That takes a minute or two (longer for Godiva's Run k_eff mode, which processes at NJOY's full tolerance).

Research, education and V&V only.

Methods and data: transport follows OpenMC (Romano et al., 2015), cross sections are processed by an NJOY port (MacFarlane et al., 2017) from ENDF/B-VIII.0 (Brown & others, 2018), Godiva and the other benchmarks are ICSBEP cases (Briggs et al., 2003), delta tracking follows Leppänen (Leppänen, 2010) (Leppänen, 2017), chord-length sampling (Liang et al., 2013) (Tan et al., 2025), ring-RPT (Kim & Baek, 2005) (Lou et al., 2020), and the HTR-10 bed uses its published benchmark data (Li et al., 2014) (Terry et al., 2005) (International Atomic Energy Agency, 2003). Software: (Ong, 2026) (Ong et al., 2026) (Ong & Xiao, 2026).
