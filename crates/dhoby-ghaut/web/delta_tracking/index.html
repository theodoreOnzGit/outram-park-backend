Then ENDF/B-VIII.0 nuclear data are downloaded and processed in this tab by OUTRAM PARK's own NJOY port, in a background worker so the page stays responsive, and the delta-tracking majorant is built. That takes a minute or two.

Research, education and V&V only.

Methods and data: delta tracking follows Leppänen (Leppänen, 2010) (Leppänen, 2017); transport follows OpenMC (Romano et al., 2015); cross sections come from ENDF/B-VIII.0 (Brown & others, 2018), processed by an NJOY port (MacFarlane et al., 2017).
