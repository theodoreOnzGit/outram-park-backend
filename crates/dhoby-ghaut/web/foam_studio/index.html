Put a pipe or a duct in a 3-D view, tag its inlet, outlet and walls, mesh it, and run OUTRAM PARK's port of OpenFOAM's pimpleFoam on it, live in a background worker in this tab: velocity and pressure on two slices, the residual history, a probe, and the velocity profile against Hagen–Poiseuille.

Research, education and V&V only. Not for reactor operation, licensing, safety decisions or emergency response.

Methods: the solver follows OpenFOAM's pimpleFoam (Weller et al., 1998); the pipe is checked against Hagen–Poiseuille flow and the development length of Durst et al. (2005); the helical coil section's friction factor against the laminar coil correlation of Mishra & Gupta (1979), as a function of the Dean number.
