# TetGen port, stage 3: PLC surface meshing against an instrumented upstream (gh:#1792)

> AI-assisted draft, untrusted until human-reviewed (`RESPONSIBLE_USE.md`).
> For education, research and V&V only.

Epic gh:#1789. Stage 3 ports what TetGen 1.6.0's `tetrahedralize()` runs
under `-p` up to and including `meshsurface()`:

- **PLC input** (`src/tetgen/plc.rs`): `tetgenio::load_poly` for `.poly` and
  `.smesh` (facets, polygons, facet holes, facet markers, volume holes,
  regions), `load_edge`, and a separate `.node`. The scanning helpers
  (`readnumberline`, `findnextnumber`, `strtol` base 0, `strtod`) are
  emulated. `PlcInput::from_triangle_soup` builds a PLC from a triangulated
  surface with one marker per triangle; `to_smesh` writes one.
- **Surface data structure** (`src/tetgen/mesh.rs`): the subface and
  subsegment pools (`MemoryPool`, upstream's allocation and LIFO
  dead-item order, including the dead-item link written into `sh[0]`),
  `ShellRecord` (upstream's eleven words plus marker, flags and facet
  index), `Face` and the encoded `shellface` pointer, `FlipShItem`.
- **`meshsurface`** (`src/tetgen/surface.rs`) and everything it calls:
  `makeindex2pointmap`, per facet the vertex and segment lists,
  `triangulate` (`calculateabovepoint`, `sinsertvertex` with the
  Bowyer-Watson cavity and the hull extension, `slocate`,
  `sscoutsegment`, `flip22`, `flipshpush`, `lawsonflip`, `scarveholes`),
  `unifysegments` (`makepoint2submap`, the face rings ordered by
  `facedihedral`), `identifyinputedges`, `mergefacets`, the ridge vertex
  types and `insegments`; the geometry they use (`facenormal`,
  `incircle3d`, `circumsphere` with `lu_decmp` / `lu_solve`, `triarea`,
  `calculateabovepoint4`).

**What the `-p` path does before `meshsurface`.** `initializepools`,
`transfernodes` and `incrementaldelaunay`, unchanged from stage 2: the PLC's
vertices are inserted with `insert_vertex_bw` exactly as a point set's are.
No `insertpoint` and no 3D flip (`flip23`, `flip32`, `flip41`, `flipnm`) runs
before or inside `meshsurface`, which works on the subfaces only, so none is
ported here. `markacutevertices` does not exist in 1.6.0.

**Branches not ported** (none reached with default switches on this call
path; each is named in the module doc of `surface.rs`): `sinsertvertex`'s
`splitseg` and `INSTAR` branches (**ported in stage 4, gh:#1793**,
`sinsertvertex_full`) (`triangulate` passes `NULL` and
`bowywat = 1`); `flip22`'s `chkencflag` queues (always 0 here); `flip31` and
`sremovevertex` (not called); the `reporterrorflag` printouts of
`sscoutsegment`; area and length bounds (`checkconstraints`, `-q` with
constraint lists); the `.stl` branch of `meshsurface`; 2D input and `-s` uv
parameters in the reader. Upstream's warnings are kept in
`TetgenMesh::warnings`.

## Methodology

- **Reference.** TetGen 1.6.0 (libigl/tetgen `e05aca7df74e`) built as its
  `makefile` builds it (g++ 13.3.0, `tetgen.cxx` `-O3`, `predicates.cxx`
  `-O0`, x86_64, glibc 2.39) plus `-ffp-contract=off`, with
  `meshsurface_dump.patch` applied: a member `dump_meshsurface()` that
  `tetrahedralize()` calls right after `meshsurface()` returns when
  `TG_DUMP=<file>` is set, then exits. Without `TG_DUMP` the instrumented
  build writes the same `example.1.{node,ele,face,edge}` as the unmodified
  binary (checked; only the `# Generated by` comment differs).
- **The dump** (text, one record per line), in pool traversal order and
  **including dead slots**: every point (slot, mark, type, flag bits,
  coordinates as bits, a duplicate's parent); `dummypoint`'s coordinates
  (the last facet's above point); every subface (three vertices in order,
  marker, flag bits, three neighbour links with edge versions, three
  segment links, two tetrahedron links); every subsegment (two vertices,
  the `NULL` third, marker, flags, its three links, whether `sh[6]` is
  set); every tetrahedron (four vertices, four neighbour links with
  versions, marker word, segment and subface arrays); the three dead-item
  stacks top first; `recentsh`; `recenttet`; the `randomnation` seed;
  `insegments`; `minfacetdihed` as bits; `flippool` items; the next glibc
  `rand()`. Pointers are printed as pool slots. This is the whole state
  stage 4 (`recoverboundary`) starts from.
- **Port.** `TetgenMesh::dump_meshsurface()` writes the same format.
- **Pass criterion.** The two dumps are equal line for line (exact text, so
  every coordinate bit, link, version and order). No tolerance.
- **Test.** `tests/tetgen_meshsurface_vs_upstream.rs` reads only committed
  files (`test_data/tetgen_meshsurface/`, gzip, 585 kB): 13 inputs, one
  `.edge`, the 13 upstream dumps, and `fin_crash.poly`.
- **Upstream-free check**
  (`surface_covers_facets_and_segments_cover_polygon_edges`): on every case
  the subfaces' total area equals the input facets' (polygon area, minus
  inner polygons where the facet has holes) within 1e-12 relative, and
  every input polygon edge is either a chain of collinear subsegments or,
  where `mergefacets` removed it between two coplanar facets of one marker,
  an edge shared by an even number of subfaces.

### Cases

| case | what it exercises |
|---|---|
| `example` | upstream's `example.poly`: facet holes (`scarveholes`' hole location), firstnumber 1 |
| `unit_box` | six quadrilateral facets |
| `box_hole` | a box with a box-shaped cavity and a volume hole |
| `box_edges` | `.edge` input: an edge already a segment, a new segment on a facet edge, a dangling edge, a degenerate edge; a duplicated vertex substituted in a facet (`dupverts`) |
| `comb_prism` | a prism with a comb cap (7 slots 0.1 wide) and 7 collinear vertices on one edge: hull insertion, `ONEDGE` insertion, degenerate-face removal, coplanar side facets merged |
| `grid_box` | octagonal faces (collinear triples) with a 3 x 3 grid of interior facet vertices: cocircular and collinear insertions, rounding in `slocate` |
| `jitter_soup` | a box as a triangle soup of non-Delaunay grid triangulations: `mergefacets` then 47 `lawsonflip` flips, `flip22` at face rings with segments |
| `slit_box` | an interior two-vertex polygon across a jittered point grid: segment recovery by `sscoutsegment` (7 `ACROSSEDGE` flips) |
| `fin_box` | an internal fin: three facets at a segment (`facedihedral` ordering in `unifysegments`) |
| `pipe`, `fuel_channel`, `pebble` | Mesh Studio's `reactor_surface` at size 2 as `.smesh` soups, one marker per patch (inlet, outlet, walls / pin / pebble, channel) |
| `sphere` | a 24 x 48 UV sphere, two hemisphere markers: `mergefacets`' small-angle removal |

Branch coverage was counted with temporary traces (since removed); the
paths above are each reached at least once.

### Regenerating

```bash
S=<scratch>/tetgen_stage3; mkdir -p $S/src $S/cases
cp <tetgen-clone>/{tetgen.cxx,tetgen.h,predicates.cxx} $S/src/
cd $S/src && patch -p1 < <repo>/crates/outram-park-fork-cfmesh/verification_and_validation/tetgen_meshsurface_gh1792/meshsurface_dump.patch
g++ -O0 -c predicates.cxx -o predicates.o
g++ -O3 -ffp-contract=off -o tetgen_dump tetgen.cxx predicates.o -lm
cd <repo> && GH1792_OUT=$S/cases cargo test --release -p outram-park-fork-cfmesh \
  --test tetgen_meshsurface_vs_upstream -- --ignored write_inputs
cp <tetgen-clone>/example.poly $S/cases/
cd $S/cases && for c in example unit_box box_hole box_edges comb_prism grid_box jitter_soup \
  slit_box fin_box pipe fuel_channel pebble sphere; do
  f=$c.poly; [ -f $f ] || f=$c.smesh; TG_DUMP=$c.dump ../src/tetgen_dump -pQ $f; done
T=<repo>/crates/outram-park-fork-cfmesh/test_data/tetgen_meshsurface
for f in *.poly *.smesh *.edge *.dump; do gzip -n -9 -c $f > $T/$f.gz; done
cargo test --release -p outram-park-fork-cfmesh --test tetgen_meshsurface_vs_upstream -- --nocapture
```

Regeneration is deterministic: rerunning the instrumented build on every
input gave byte-identical dumps.

**13 of 13 cases identical, 39 207 of 39 207 dump lines:** 2 708 points,
5 358 subfaces, 4 852 subsegments and 15 205 tetrahedra in pool order with
every link and version, every dead slot and dead-item stack, `recentsh`,
`recenttet`, the random-number state and `minfacetdihed` (so `acos` agrees
with glibc's on these inputs). No difference was found, so there is none to
explain.

**Where upstream crashes.** `fin_crash.poly` (the fin with its edges inside
two side facets rather than on their boundaries) makes the instrumented
build stop with a segmentation fault in `unifysegments`
(`testseg.sh[3]` through a `NULL` segment, upstream `tetgen.cxx:14187`, found
with gdb on a `-O0 -g` build). The port returns
`TetgenError::InternalError` at the same read
(`upstream_crash_is_an_internal_error`).

**The comparison can fail.** Setting `flip22`'s `recentsh` to
`flipfaces[1]` instead of `flipfaces[0]` makes `jitter_soup` differ at dump
line 1 320 (`recentsh f0.5` against `f1.1`); tried and reverted. Swapping
`sscoutsegment`'s random choice in its `(--)` case changed nothing: the
branch is reached once (`comb_prism`), the draw is consumed either way, and
both moves end on the same edge there (recorded, not hidden: that one choice
is not pinned by these cases).
`the_comparison_can_fail` also shows the port with `-M` (`nomergefacet`)
differs from upstream's default dump on `pebble`.

**Upstream-free check:** all 13 cases cover their facets' area exactly
(to the printed digits; the bound is 1e-12 relative) and every polygon edge
is a segment chain or a merged coplanar edge (e.g. `pebble`: 4 140 chains,
2 196 merged).

### Run time (x86_64, release; wall clock)

Upstream: its own "Delaunay seconds" and "Surface mesh seconds"
(`tetgen -pNEF`, unmodified binary), three runs. Port: the test's timings of
`transfernodes` + `incrementaldelaunay` and of `meshsurface`, three runs.

| case | upstream Delaunay (ms) | port Delaunay (ms) | upstream meshsurface (ms) | port meshsurface (ms) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `example` | 0.15–0.22 | 0.18 | 0.07–0.10 | 0.10–0.11 |
| `comb_prism` | 0.49–0.70 | 0.61–0.63 | 0.25–0.34 | 0.25–0.26 |
| `pebble` | 64–73 | 57–73 | 1.5–2.2 | 1.5–1.7 |
| `sphere` | 54–67 | 50–65 | 1.7–2.1 | 1.7–2.0 |

The port is within upstream's run-to-run scatter. The Delaunay phase
dominates on `pebble` and `sphere` (cospherical vertex rings: the exact
`insphere` and its symbolic perturbation), in both codes.
