About this model

Colours: fluid temperature, 20 °C (blue) to 100 °C (red), the desktop simulator's map. Arrows: flow direction from the sign of each branch's flow; the DRACS solve returns a magnitude, drawn in the direction the model assumes (up through the DHX tubes). Nothing moves on its own here: the simulator's state does not carry the fluid inventory that a tracer's speed would need.

The physics is a faithful port of the v1 simulator, whose model the maintainer validated; the equivalence of v1 and v2 has not been verified. Calibrated parasitic heat losses and controller gains are v1's. The heater trips at 150 °C at its inlet or outlet, 160 °C in its fluid or 350 °C in its shell, as on the desktop.

Research, education and V&V only. An offline demonstration: never connected to a plant, not for reactor control, licensing or safety decisions.
