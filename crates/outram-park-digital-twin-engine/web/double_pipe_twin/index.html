A digital twin of a laminar double-pipe heat exchanger: OUTRAM PARK's TUAS 1-D model, evaluated live in a background worker in this tab, corrected toward a conjugate-heat-transfer CFD by a Kennedy–O'Hagan calibration with a discrepancy δ(x). The calibration, the co-kriging sweep and the CFD runs were computed beforehand and are shown as committed.

Research, education and V&V only. Not for reactor operation, licensing, safety decisions or emergency response.
