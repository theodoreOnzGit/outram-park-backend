## `htr10_equilibrium_core_inventory.csv`

HTR-10 **equilibrium-core fission-product inventory**, 22 nuclides, in
becquerels. This is the quantity
`physics/fission_product_release.rs` names as the one it does not have — its
TRISO-ATOPS channel reports every activity **per curie of core inventory**
because "nothing in this module derives an inventory". All five nuclides that
channel tracks (`Kr-85`, `Xe-133`, `I-131`, `Cs-137`, `Ag-110m`) are in this
table.

### Why copying these 22 numbers is in policy while the PDF is not

The private corpus's own README draws the line: *"Research knowledge about
them (citations, notes, connections) may still live in public Kovan
libraries; the documents and their extracted full text may not."* A cited
table of 22 published values is `DATA_POLICY.md`'s "public literature data"
— normal scientific citation of a journal article — not the document and not
its extracted full text. **Do not add the PDF, or bulk extracted text from
it, to this repository.**

### Processing / digitisation steps

1. The maintainer transcribed Table 1 into a `csv` fenced block in the Kovan
   annotation, region-anchored to page 3 of the PDF.
2. That block was copied here **verbatim** — all 22 values `diff`-checked
   identical to the Kovan source on 2026-09-23.
3. The only change is the header row: `Nuclide,Inventory (Bq)` was rewritten
   as `nuclide,inventory_bq` to match this directory's snake_case convention
   (`baseline_default_commands.csv`). No value, ordering or significant
   figure was altered.

### Not verified

These values have **not** been checked against the published Table 1 by
anyone in this repository — the step-1 transcription is trusted as the
maintainer's own. They have not been reproduced by any calculation here, and
no consistency check (e.g. against fission yields at 80 000 MWd/t) has been
run. Treat as AI-relayed literature data pending human review, per
`RESPONSIBLE_USE.md`.

### Intended use

Offline, educational demonstration only. `RESPONSIBLE_USE.md` applies in
full: nothing derived from this table may be quoted as a source term for
HTR-10 or any other real plant.
