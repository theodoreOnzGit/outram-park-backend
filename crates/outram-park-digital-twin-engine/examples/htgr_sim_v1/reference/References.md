## `htr10_equilibrium_core_inventory.csv` — MOVED 2026-09-24

~~HTR-10 equilibrium-core fission-product inventory, 22 nuclides, in
becquerels.~~ **The table now lives in `changi`**, at
`crates/changi/reference/htr10_equilibrium_core_inventory.csv`, exposed by
`changi::activity::inventory`, with its full provenance record in
`crates/changi/docs/References.md`.

It was briefly duplicated here. Two copies of a published table is the drift
this workspace forbids, and `changi::activity` is the right home because the
atmospheric-dispersion channel downstream is its consumer —
`physics::fission_product_release` now reads it from there rather than
carrying its own.

Source, unchanged: Liu Yuanzhong and Cao Jianzhu, *Nuclear Engineering and
Design* **218** (2002) 81–90, Table 1, ORIGEN2 at 80 000 MWd/t. Restricted
access; the table is cited, the document is not redistributed.
