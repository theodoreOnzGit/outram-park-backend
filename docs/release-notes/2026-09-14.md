## Two new crates

### `petir` — core numerics, `no_std`

**P**olynomials, **E**quations, **T**ransforms, **I**ntegration and **R**oots.
A dependency-lean numerics core, ported rather than written: GSL's Chebyshev
module, the exponential integral `E₁`, and the incomplete gamma functions, each
checked code-to-code against GSL itself. `tampines-steam-tables`' Chebyshev
evaluator is now a thin re-export of it, so the two cannot drift.

Late in the window it also grew a **fast, deterministic `exp`/`ln`/`powf`**,
transcribed from ARM optimized-routines — the implementation glibc itself
ships. Each is verified **100.000 % bit-identical** to upstream's own compiled
C over committed oracles (8 290 / 16 748 / 42 176 probes), and all three are
now `petir::real`'s default route. Measured from Rust, 2 M calls per route,
five runs:

| | old `libm` route → ARM route | ARM route / platform |
|---|---|---|
| `exp` | 1.75 – 1.86× faster | 0.70 – 0.75× (*faster than glibc*) |
| `ln` | 1.09 – 1.18× faster | 1.04 – 1.25× |
| `powf` | 2.09 – 2.18× faster | 1.41 – 1.57× |

**Breaking, in the last bits.** ARM's `exp` and musl's are different
implementations, so a default build's `exp`/`ln`/`powf` output moved once.
Anything pinned to full `f64` precision needs re-baselining. Nothing is less
accurate — all three are within ~1 ulp of the platform and now identical on
*every* platform, which they were not before. Every PETIR consumer's suite was
re-run and passes; `tampines-steam-tables`' generated V&V reports came back
numerically identical to the character.

### `farrer-park` — FEM structural mechanics

**F**inite-element **A**nalysis for **R**eactor **R**eliability, **E**ngineering
**R**esponse, **P**lasticity **A**nd **R**isk. Ported from MOOSE,
PRISMS-Plasticity and PRISMS-Fatigue. Small-strain linear elasticity and J2
plasticity with a consistent tangent on Lagrange Tri3/Tri6/Quad4/Tet4/Hex8,
plus **rate-dependent crystal plasticity** (FCC {111}⟨110⟩ and BCC {110}⟨111⟩),
**B-bar** mean-dilatation and **plane stress**.

Verification only — MMS orders match theory, patch test at machine precision,
Newton order 2.004, Schmid yield to 1.5e-15, frame indifference 7.6e-16. **No
validation, and it is not an RPV or piping life-assessment tool.** Known and
stated gaps: shear locking is *measured and not cured* (11.25 % too stiff at
two Quad4 through the depth, 66.7 % at aspect ratio 4), ILU(0) stagnates on
nearly incompressible systems, no curved elements, and the fatigue work is
partial (Fatemi–Socie indicator only, no life prediction).

## Nuclear data — `njoy-outram-park-fork`

The largest single block of work in the release: **+45 057 / −6 627 lines**
across 205 files. The NJOY2016 module set is now substantially complete and
each piece is gated against NJOY2016's own output, built from source in-tree
rather than trusted from documentation.

**Modules ported and oracle-matched this window**

- **GROUPR** — two-body elastic matrices (U-238 MF=6/MT=2 to 5.5e-6),
  P0–P3 matrices, discrete-level inelastic (MT=51/52/60/89), MT=257/258/259
  derived quantities, `genflx` heterogeneity and multi-moderator terms, MF=10
  residual-production yield search, URR self-shielding.
- **LEAPR** — whole-deck run driver byte-identical to three NJOY2016 runs,
  Sköld coherence (`skold`) verified on D-in-D₂O, mixed-moderator merge
  verified on SiO₂ and H₂O, cold-hydrogen path.
- **ERRORR** — the MF=33 path end-to-end against nine NJOY2016 tapes; MF=32
  LRF=7 `rpxsamm` + SAMM resonance-parameter derivatives to **4.8e-7** on
  ENDF/B-VII.1 Cl-35; the `resprx` chain matched on TENDL-2023 Ar-37.
- **DTFR** — full-channel assembly (ν̄σ_f, χ including constant-spectrum and
  delayed, χ_d/ν_d, P1 and photon tables) matching NJOY2016 CLAW output;
  golden test on U-238, 32×29 to six figures.
- **RESXSR** — byte-identical to NJOY2016 at two temperatures, after fixing
  gfortran record framing and `locm`.
- **MIXR**, **WIMSR**, **GAMINR**, **COVR** — each with its own oracle; COVR's
  library option byte-identical on nine materials.

**Defects found and fixed** — several were real physics errors, not cosmetics:

- `RECONR` evaluated LRF=2 with the SLBW formula instead of `csmlbw`'s
  multilevel elastic. Ar-37 elastic below 100 eV went from **+6.3 %** to within
  **4e-7** of NJOY2016 tape21.
- `WAVE_K` was rounded up (`cwaven`), and `unresx`'s AMUN fold was wrong.
- `BROADR`'s `erfc` was single-precision; H-2 capture is no longer 2 % low at
  1e-5 eV.
- `getdis` used the wrong `nld` quadrature order; `getfle` slid a stale
  coefficient.
- Zhu's exact per-atom-type Debye–Waller option implemented for coherent
  elastic; the SiC coherent-elastic residual was traced to the *published
  evaluation's* atomic basis, not to this port.

**`gh:#188` closed.** The thermal-scattering emission matrix the Monte Carlo
sampled was `equiprobable_emission` — written here, never a port of anything.
It is replaced by a port of `aceth.f90`'s `acesix`. Measured on
`outram-mc-libs`' stationary-distribution oracle, an instrument independent of
any table comparison, graphite's shape error against a Maxwellian went from
**−1.45 % to −0.11 %** — equal to the free-gas control's −0.10 %.

## Monte Carlo transport — `outram-mc-libs`

**+39 161 / −963 lines** across 121 files, and the bulk of it is measurement
rather than new machinery.

**Criticality benchmark campaign.** Three ICSBEP cases were brought up and the
residuals attributed rather than reported:

| benchmark | result |
|---|---|
| HEU-SOL-THERM-009 (thermal) | −18 ± 171 pcm |
| Jemima (U-238-dominated fast) | +6 ± 173 pcm |
| LEU-COMP-THERM-008 | **+2 950 ± 61 pcm**, root-caused to `gh:#188` |

The LCT-008 hunt is worth reading as a method: resonance escape, the majorant,
resonance shape, the elastic angular law and the graphite thermal law were each
priced by deleting or perturbing them, a positive control was added to the
pricing harness, and the residual was localised to the H-in-H₂O thermal
*kernel* moving neutrons 2–5.5 % too little. Godiva's long-standing +57 pcm
baseline turned out to be **one seed's draw**, not the code's answer.

**Doubly heterogeneous media.** One enum (`DhTreatment`) now selects how a DH
universe is resolved, with k-eff V&V across every treatment on ENDF/B-VIII.0.
The final run — 7 200 × [15 inactive + 40 active], FHR reference unit cell,
crystalline-graphite S(α,β) at 600 K, single-threaded on every arm, combined
standard error ~320 pcm — settles the question:

| treatment | k | vs exact | speed |
|---|---|--:|--:|
| delta tracking (exact) | 1.38647 ± 0.00211 | — | 1.00× |
| **ring-RPT (fitted annulus)** | 1.38684 ± 0.00252 | **+37 pcm (0.1 σ)** | 2.13× |
| chord-length sampling | 1.35380 ± 0.00267 | −3 267 pcm (9.6 σ) | 2.16× |
| semi-implicit CLS | 1.35282 ± 0.00228 | −3 365 pcm (10.8 σ) | 2.09× |
| naive homogenisation | 1.34345 ± 0.00234 | −4 302 pcm (13.7 σ) | 1.75× |

Ring-RPT is statistically indistinguishable from exact delta tracking at
statistics that would have resolved 1 000 pcm comfortably; filling the same
zone uniformly costs a *resolved* −4 302 pcm. The independent check agrees in
magnitude: the OpenMC deck this geometry comes from measures its own ring-RPT
at −31 pcm (0.34 σ) from its own explicit pebble.

Two earlier claims were **retracted on measurement** in reaching that: both
"speedups" are slowdowns for eigenvalue problems, and until 14 September
`DhTreatment::RingRpt` was reporting naive homogenisation as ring-RPT — the
two are ~4 340 pcm apart.

**Physics fixes** (each with a GitHub issue): a signed surface token carried
through crossings so concentric-sphere transport no longer leaks (`#168`);
absorption is MT=27 — capture + charged-particle emission + fission — not just
capture + fission (`#169`); real Σ_a on `MacroXs`; explicit per-energy leakage
accounting in the CSG eigenvalue path.

**New capability.** Six-factor and lethargy-spectrum capture
(`physics::reactor_physics`), tally scoring and multigroup transport, FHR
ring-RPT pebble builders wired to real CSG geometry with graphite S(α,β), and
optional `wgpu` GPU compute for batched pointwise cross-section interpolation.

## Finite volume and meshing

**`outram-foam-basic-lib`** — WGSL compute kernels for the LDU sparse product,
vector ops and closed-form polynomial roots; the Krylov solvers and
preconditioners run on a hybrid CPU/GPU backend; a data-level co-execution
planner decides placement (GitHub `#18`, `#11`).

**`outram-park-fork-cfmesh`** — a working tet-dual pipeline end to end:
tetrahedralization, flip-based Delaunay improvement, polyhedral (median) dual,
face-minimal merging with smart-Laplacian smoothing, prism and adaptive
boundary layers for curved walls, multi-level octree refinement with 2:1
balancing, per-surface patch separation, `polyMeshGenChecks`-equivalent quality
checks, an OpenFOAM `polyMesh` writer, and **Mesh Studio**, an egui front end.
Reactor-shaped examples ship with it: LWR pin lattice, pebble bed, MSR channel,
coolant-around-pebble.

**`outram-blender`** — 70 commits completing the `op-hzs.54.*` mesh-authoring
series: the full transform toolset with proportional editing and a snapping
engine, dissolve/delete matrix, bridge and fill operators, generate and deform
modifier stacks with correct stack semantics, voxel remesh, normals toolset and
attribute layers, curve authoring, curve↔mesh and NURBS surfaces, text→geometry,
LoopTools, Snap Utilities Line, Precision Drawing Tools, Bool Tool, and array
patterns (radial / object-offset / along-curve).

**`tampines`** — a 1-D **six-equation two-fluid solver** with interfacial
exchange closures, alongside the drift-flux solver; a pebble-bed conduction
stack with a helium gas layer; `raffles` wired in for real-time-via-surrogate.

**`tampines-steam-tables`** — a mean-flow stage-by-stage turbine model, a
transient mean-flow turbine on the 1-D HEM KNP hybrid, the shaft loop closed
through the generator, experimental Chebyshev backward correlations (now on
PETIR), and diagnostics that emit **markdown V&V reports**, committed.

**`outram-park-fork-coolprop`** — 126 incompressible fluids and 840 mixture
binary pairs generated from upstream data; humid-air entropy with wet-bulb and
dew-point as input keys; an exact backward `T(ρ, h)` seeded from the
fixed-pressure solve then Newton-corrected. Two boundary-condition defects in
`OPCPFluidArray` fixed, bounded scalar convection and a conservative pEqn flux
added.

## Known limitations carried into this release

Stated plainly rather than left to be discovered:

- `farrer-park`'s shear locking is measured and **not** cured; ILU(0) stagnates
  on nearly incompressible systems; no curved elements.
- `outram-park-fork-dwsim-libs`' `RefluxedAbsorber` is not solvable.
- `outram-park-fork-liggghts`' granular physics is **not** validated, only its
  integrator and contact laws.
- `bedok` carries no parity claim pending rebuilt benchmark coverage
  (`op-swhd`).
- `outram-park-fork-moltres`, `-onix` and `-thermochimica` are AI-assisted
  drafts with no human V&V, and nothing depends on them yet.
- PETIR's `ln` gains only ~1.1× over the `libm` route it replaces — adopted for
  determinism, not speed.
- A deliberate divergence from ARM upstream: `__math_invalid` returns
  `(x−x)/(x−x)`, whose NaN sign is architecture-dependent. PETIR returns the
  positive quiet NaN on every target, because a platform-dependent NaN would
  defeat the determinism the module exists for.
