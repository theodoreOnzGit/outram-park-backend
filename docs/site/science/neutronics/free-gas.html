Maths 2: the free gas, exactly

Neutronics

Rung 1½'s band can be written down exactly. For a gas of free atoms (NJOY2016 manual, eqs. 538–539 (MacFarlane et al., 2017)):

S(α, β) = exp[−(α + β)² / 4α] / √(4πα)

  • Centred on the recoil line: the exponent is zero at β = −α.
  • A bell in β whose width grows with the kick: its spread is √(2α).
  • Point at the exp: this is the bell curve from rung 1½, the Maxwell–Boltzmann velocities seen through the kick.

The free-gas band

Cool it towards 0 K and the band shrinks back to rung 1's line. Two checks the formula passes, and every scattering law must: the chances over all β add up to 1, and on average the neutron loses exactly the free recoil energy: the mean of β is −α. That second one is called the sum rule, and it will hold on every rung.

Conventions on these pages. β = (E′ − E)/kT is positive when the neutron gains energy; α measures the size of the kick. S is LEAPR's asymmetric scattering law (the NJOY2016 manual's script S); ENDF stores the symmetric S = eβ/2 times it (page 21).
