Maths 5e: why is it a bell curve?

Neutronics

The atom's speed along any direction is bell-shaped, for two reasons:

  • Thermal jiggle: the Maxwell–Boltzmann distribution, a Gaussian (rung 1½).
  • Quantum jiggle: a swing in its ground state also has a Gaussian spread of momentum, the shape of the oscillator's ground state. So rung 5b's "the ground state still has motion" literally produces a bell curve.

A bell-shaped spread of atom speeds gives a bell-shaped spread of energy losses (rung 5d's shift is a straight-line function of the speed). That is why the curve is a bell.
