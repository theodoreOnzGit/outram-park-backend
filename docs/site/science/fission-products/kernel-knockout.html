Complication 2: knockout

Fission products

Recoil was the fragment itself escaping. Knockout is a fragment, or its cascade (lesson 5), hitting atoms that are already sitting near the surface and kicking them out. Like recoil, it needs no heat, so it matters most when the fuel is cold and diffusion is slow.
