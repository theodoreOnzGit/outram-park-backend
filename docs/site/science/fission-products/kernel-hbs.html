Complication 8: the high-burnup structure

Fission products

After enough fissions, the damaged lattice does something drastic: it restructures. The original grains break up into very small new grains, with many small pores between them. This is the high-burnup structure. Small grains mean short paths to a boundary.

How restructuring bends the clean curve

SCIANTIX models its formation as a restructuring law in the “effective burnup”, with the Barani et al. (2020) option, and tracks the restructured volume fraction (Pizzocri et al., 2018), high_burnup_structure_formation.rst. Whether and where it forms in TRISO kernels, and the size of its new grains: source pending. Link back: in the many-spheres model, a share of the spheres becomes much smaller.
