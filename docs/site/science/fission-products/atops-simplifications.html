Why simplify at all

Because the top of the ladder cannot run for a core. A finite-element particle for every one of the roughly 2×108 particles of HTR-10 is out of reach (theory book step 6), and lesson 32 found that even a random walk through one particle’s layers takes too long. Each rung down keeps less physics and costs less. A simplification is acceptable when what it drops does not change the answer you need, and every row above says what it drops.

The next rung up that the workspace lacks is a multilayer diffusion solver, FRESCO-style: the diffusion equation solved through kernel, coatings and graphite on a mesh, with decay, along a temperature history (theory book step 9). It would replace the equivalent sphere and the “intact holds everything” rule, and it is the rung lesson 32’s exact mean could check.
