Complication 3: grain-boundary sweeping

Fission products

In lesson 12 the grains grew. When a boundary moves into a grain, the material it passes becomes part of the boundary region, and whatever that material held is now at the boundary, without having diffused there. The boundary sweeps it up.

How sweeping bends the clean curve

SCIANTIX models this: as the grain radius grows, the gas in the swept shell is moved from the grain to the boundary (Pizzocri et al., 2018), grain_boundary_sweeping.rst. Its documentation adds a surprise: it applies sweeping to helium only, and deliberately not to xenon and krypton. Link back: in the many-spheres model, the spheres would grow with time.
