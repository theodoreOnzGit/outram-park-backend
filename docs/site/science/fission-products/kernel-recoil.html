Complication 1: recoil

Fission products

Lessons 16 to 19 had every atom start at rest and diffuse. But a fission product is born flying (lesson 4). If it is born within one fragment range of the kernel’s surface and heads outward, it is out before diffusion starts: a recoil skin.

How recoil bends the clean curve

How thick is the skin? The fragment range in UO2: the RustBCA record gives … for the heavy fragment (illustrative; the RustBCA tracks are pending gh:#979). The share that escapes is worked out geometrically by the page (lesson 19’s flat line). Recoil adds a release that does not wait for time or temperature, and does not depend on half-life. Link back: the many-spheres model plus this constant.

Learn more advanced stuff

The page counts the escaping share by Monte Carlo: uniform births in the sphere, isotropic straight flights of one range, and counts those that end outside. For a range much smaller than the radius it is about three quarters of the range over the radius. Within a grain the same happens at every grain boundary, but there it only moves the atom into the next grain.
