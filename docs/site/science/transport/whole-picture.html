The pebble column mixes two things on purpose, and says which: the Watch demo is the two-dimensional analogue (rods, reflective cell, tracks only), the record is the three-dimensional fuel-zone cell with spherical kernels. The columns for the pebble and the core describe what the repository's demos and records do (crates/dhoby-ghaut/examples/monte_carlo_web, htr10/core/mod.rs: "bed delta-tracked against bed_majorant, everything else" by surface tracking), not every possible way to run them.
