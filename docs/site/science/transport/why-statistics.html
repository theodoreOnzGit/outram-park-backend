Why can the error bar be trusted?

Transport

A k_eff run ends with a line such as "k = 0.99994 ± 0.00050". What justifies the second number?

The argument

  1. Each active generation gives an estimate kgen (page 9), which is itself an average over thousands of histories, so it is the sum of many small contributions. By the central limit theorem it is close to normally distributed (the same argument that gives any Monte Carlo estimate an error bar; for the general theory of Monte Carlo estimates and their errors in transport see (Lux & Koblinger, 1991)).
  2. Once the source has converged (the entropy has stopped drifting, page 9), every active generation starts from a source of the same shape, so the kgen are identically distributed.
  3. If they were also independent, the mean of the n active ones would have standard error s/√n, with s their sample standard deviation. That is what the code reports (mean_and_stderr over the active generations).

Step 3 is the weak link, and the code's own documentation says so: the generations are not independent, because each generation's source is built from the one before. The correlation usually makes the naive error bar too small. The statistics module (stats) computes batch means and an integrated autocorrelation time beside the run's own error, never replacing it, and by the maintainer's decision of 2026-10-03 every gate on it is still recorded as not yet measured (CLAUDE.md of outram-mc-libs, "Statistics on top of RAFFLES"). There is therefore no measured value of our code's inter-generation correlation to quote on this page.

Predict first: if neighbouring generations are positively correlated at 0.5, by what factor is the error bar that assumes independence too small?

What ignoring correlation costs

An illustration of the arithmetic, not a measurement of OutramMC: generation-to-generation correlation is modelled by an AR(1) sequence whose correlation you set. For lag-one correlation ρ the actual scatter of a long run’s mean exceeds the error bar that assumes independence by about √((1+ρ)/(1−ρ)); check-transport.js simulates 1500 runs at ρ = 0.5 and finds a ratio of 1.76 against 1.73 (2026-10-18).

The answer: at a correlation of 0.5 the actual scatter is about 1.7 times the quoted error. Note the direction: a positive correlation is the usual case, and it makes the quoted ± optimistic.

What the repository has measured instead: many seeds

The honest check is empirical: run the same case with many independent seeds and compare the scatter of their k with the error each run quoted. The Godiva example does this and reports a seed-consistency statistic (internal error against seed-to-seed scatter, gh:#494: the spread_ratio is the seed-to-seed standard deviation divided by the rms internal error, and is about 1 when the internal error is honest). Its record, at 5000 neutrons × [40 inactive + 120 active] (examples/godiva_keff_ensemble.rs):

EnsembleMean offset from the benchmarkOne run scatters byχ2/dof of internal error against scatter
1024 seeds, 2026-10-05, 6faff1ed8 (the current record)−6 ± 5 pcm165 pcm0.97 (dof 1023; 95 % band 0.92 to 1.09): consistent
512 seeds, same day, after a source fix (gh:#527)−7 ± 8 pcm179 pcm1.17 (dof 511; band 0.88 to 1.13): flagged over-dispersed

Read it both ways. On 1024 seeds the quoted errors are consistent with the real scatter, which is what an honest error bar looks like. On the 512-seed re-run they are slightly too small (1.17 is outside its band). The record states that this is unexplained: the fix only touches the inactive generations, so it is not an expected consequence, and whether it is a 1-in-200 draw or something in how the shared machine ran is not resolved. And the known aliasing of random-number streams (page 4, gh:#700) is a candidate source of correlation that has not been measured.

What to take away

  • The ± of one run is a good first guide, and for Godiva at these settings the 1024-seed record supports it.
  • It is derived assuming independent generations, which is not true; the direction of the error is optimistic.
  • The remedy that does not depend on any assumption is many independent seeds, pooled: the ± of a pooled mean is the seed-to-seed standard deviation over √N. That is what every V&V number in this repository that carries a seed count is.
