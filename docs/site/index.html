Live demo: the Monte Carlo demo, a rung at a time

One app with a rung setting. Godiva, a bare sphere of uranium: watch real neutron histories, then run a true k-eigenvalue calculation in your browser, one console line per generation. TRISO pebble: one neutron at a time through a 2D analogue of an HTR-10 fuel pebble. Every neutron is transported by outram-mc-libs on real ENDF/B-VIII.0 data, which your browser downloads and processes with the workspace's NJOY port in a background worker before the first neutron flies — expect a minute or two (more for Godiva's Run k_eff, which processes at NJOY's full tolerance); the page stays responsive meanwhile, and on a phone the control panel folds away to the left.
